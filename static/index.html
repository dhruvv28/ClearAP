      How it works

      The AI reads the invoice. Fixed, auditable rules decide what happens to the money. Anything uncertain goes to a named person with the reason attached.

      Design decisions

      AI extracts, rules decide
      A finance team can't audit a model's judgement call, but it can audit a rule. The LLM handles the messy part (any layout, scans, bundled lines). The approve, hold or reject decision is deterministic and explainable line by line.
      Two readers, not one
      With an API key set, every invoice is read by an LLM (Gemini or Claude) and by a rule-based parser. Fields where they agree are trusted. Fields where they disagree go to a human instead of being guessed. Without a key, or if the AI call fails, the rule-based parser and OCR run alone, so the process never stops.
      Hold is the default when unsure
      A wrong approval costs money; a wrong hold costs a few minutes. Low confidence, ambiguous PO matches and new bank details all hold rather than pass.
      Every hold has an owner
      Procurement for PO problems, treasury for bank changes, the vendor for missing information (with a query drafted), the AP clerk for unreadable fields.
      A person makes the final call on holds
      The reviewer can release or reject a held invoice, but only with a note, and every action lands in the audit trail. Some holds can't be overridden: an invoice with no number, from a vendor who isn't onboarded, or that isn't an invoice at all. A rejected invoice can be resubmitted after correction without being flagged as a duplicate.
      Arithmetic breaks ties
      When the AI and the rule-based parser read different amounts, the process keeps the reading where subtotal + tax = total and the lines add up. If neither reconciles, a person checks it.

      Policy in force

      Change these to see how decisions shift. New runs use the new values, and each run records the policy it was decided under. Reset demo restores the defaults.

      Assumptions

      • The buyer is one Indian entity (Meridian Retail, Karnataka), so GST type is checked: CGST + SGST for Karnataka suppliers, IGST for everyone else.
      • Matching is 2-way (invoice to PO). Goods receipts aren't in scope; adding a GRN table would make it 3-way with the same rule structure.
      • PO balance is consumed by paid and approved invoices, on the pre-tax amount. Held invoices don't consume balance until released.
      • Blanket POs (service contracts billed monthly) are matched on overall value, not line prices.
      • An inferred PO is accepted only when one candidate clearly wins on amount and description; otherwise procurement decides.
      • A changed bank account is never auto-accepted from an invoice, even with a perfect PO match.
